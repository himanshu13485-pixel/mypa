The system

Four surfaces over one record

Nothing is bolted on and nothing syncs overnight — the client, the billing, the money and the people who did the work are the same object seen from different angles.

01

Clients

One page per client, and everything on it.

  • Leads assigned, transferred and chased to a close
  • Internal notes, attributed and timestamped
  • A client shared only with the staff you name
02

Billing

Quotation to tax invoice, without retyping it.

  • CGST, SGST, IGST and TDS worked out per line
  • A numbering series per company, with no gaps in it
  • Your letterhead, stamp and bank details on the PDF
03

Money in

Payments chased, matched and settled.

  • Payment links, and part payments against one invoice
  • A matched payment settles at once, or waits for an admin
  • Expenses, vendors and commissions on the same books
04

People

The people who did the work, on the same system.

  • Attendance and daily work reports, per employee
  • Leave requests, approvals, and a log of both
  • Salary runs and payslips, with an export for your accountant

Capabilities

Six things you stop paying for separately

Leads that do not go cold

Every lead is owned, with follow-ups logged against it. Transfer, share or reopen — the log says who did, and when.

Quotation to tax invoice

Raise a proforma and convert it when the order lands. Tax per line, a numbering series per registered company, your stamp on the PDF.

Money in, and money chased

Payment links, part payments and reminders for whatever is overdue — settled on the spot or checked by an admin, as your company works.

What the work cost you

Expenses, vendors, commissions and incentives against the same clients, so what a job earned is a report rather than an argument.

Attendance, leave and payroll

Punch and daily work reports, leave with a paid-leave account behind it, and salary runs that produce a real payslip.

Every change, on the record

Leads, invoices, payments and leave each carry a log of who changed what and when, with one user log across the lot.

Permissions enforced on the server

Roles and the per-module rights matrix are checked by middleware on every request. Hiding a button in the interface is not how access control works here.

Private by construction

Internal notes never reach the client they describe. Staff see their own book unless you widen it, and what an admin does lands in a log nobody can quietly rewrite.
