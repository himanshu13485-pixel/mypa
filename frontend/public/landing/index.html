The day book

One entry, carried through to the last column

Each stage hands the next everything it already knows, so nobody writes a client, a rate or a line item twice to move the work along.

Entry 01

An enquiry arrives

It is owned by somebody from the moment it lands, and every follow-up is written against it.

Entry 02

A quotation goes out

A proforma on your letterhead, numbered in its own series, with the tax already worked out.

Entry 03

The order is confirmed

The proforma becomes a tax invoice — the same lines, the same figures, a new number in the sales series.

iii
The ledger

An account per client, and everything posted to it

A lead becomes a client without anybody retyping it. From then on the account carries its own history — every quotation and invoice raised against it, what has been paid, the complaints logged, and the notes your team writes but the client never sees.

Leads & clients

Every lead is owned, with the follow-ups logged against it. Transfer it, share it, reopen it — the log says who did, and when.

Quotes & invoices

Raise a proforma and convert it when the order lands — the same lines, a new number in a series of its own.

Payments

Payment links, part payments against one invoice, and reminders that chase whatever is still owed.

Costs & commissions

Expenses, vendors, commissions and incentives posted against the same clients, so profit is a report and not an argument.

Attendance & leave

Punch in from the app, file a daily work report, and ask for leave in the place the leave is approved.

Payroll

Salary runs that produce a payslip from the company that actually pays it, unpaid leave already accounted for.

Sales & billing

Quotation to tax invoice, without retyping it

Raise a proforma, and convert it the moment the order is confirmed. Tax is worked out per line, and each of your registered companies counts through a numbering series of its own — so two companies billing on the same day never collide.

  • CGST, SGST, IGST and TDS worked out per line
  • A numbering series per company, with no gaps in it
  • Your letterhead, stamp and bank details on the PDF
  • Print, preview and download are the same document
